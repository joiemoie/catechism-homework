Debate Karl Marx

Karl Marx famously called religion "the opium of the people" — a false comfort that keeps the poor from rising up against an unjust world. He believed that faith was an illusion invented by the powerful to control the weak.

As future Confirmation candidates, you are called to know, live, share, and defend your faith. This game puts that to the test.

Your mission: Engage Karl Marx in debate. Use logic, Scripture, and the teachings of the Church to dismantle his arguments one by one. Avoid insults, fallacies, and weak reasoning — they will cost you credibility.

Tips before you play:

  • Read each argument carefully before responding.
  • Watch for choices marked with 💡 — those tend to be solid logical responses.
  • Choices marked with 💢 are aggressive or fallacious — they usually backfire.
  • You can try again as many times as you need. Learning from mistakes is the point.
